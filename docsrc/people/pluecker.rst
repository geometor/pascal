Julius Plücker
==============

*(1801–1868 · Germany)*

Mathematician and physicist who revolutionized analytic and projective geometry with homogeneous coordinates and line coordinates. In 1830, Plücker proved that the 20 Steiner points lie four-by-four along **15 Plücker lines**.
