Michel Chasles
==============

*(1793–1880 · France)*

French geometer who proved that four points on an irreducible conic possess an intrinsic projective cross-ratio $(P_1, P_2; P_3, P_4)_{\mathcal{C}}$ independent of the observer point $V$ chosen on the conic (*Traité des sections coniques*, 1865).
