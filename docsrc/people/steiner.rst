Jakob Steiner
=============

*(1796–1863 · Switzerland & Berlin)*

Master of synthetic projective geometry. In 1828, Steiner proved that the 60 Pascal lines generated by six conic points concur three-by-three in **20 Steiner points** (:math:`G`), with two points arising from each complementary bi-triangle partition of the vertices.
