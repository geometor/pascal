Blaise Pascal¶

The works of Blaise Pascal (1623–1662) revolutionized projective geometry with the discovery of the hexagrammum mysticum in his landmark 1640 broadside Essay pour les Coniques. Within the interconnected GEOMETOR semantic digital archive, these foundational texts are liberated from static print bounds into addressable semantic units, calibrated vector diagrams, primary-source folio provenance, and exact symbolic algebra speaking one shared geometric language.
