Pappus of Alexandria
====================

*(fl. c. 320–340 CE · Alexandria, Roman Egypt)*

Last great synthetic geometer of antiquity, author of the eight-book *Synagoge* (Mathematical Collection). Pappus proved the two-line Hexagon Theorem (*Synagoge* VII.139) thirteen centuries before Pascal showed that the two lines could be replaced by any smooth conic section.
