Charles Julien Brianchon
========================

*(1783–1864 · France)*

French geometer who in 1806 discovered Brianchon's theorem, the exact projective dual of Pascal's theorem: the three principal diagonals of a hexagon circumscribed about a conic section concur at a single point.
