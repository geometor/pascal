.. _bibliography:

============
Bibliography
============

Primary editions, foundational treatises, and scholarly literature on Blaise Pascal's *Essay pour les Coniques* (1640) and the projective geometry of the *Hexagrammum Mysticum*.

.. _cite-pascal-1640:

[pascal:1640] Essay pour les coniques
-------------------------------------

**Blaise Pascal**, *Essay pour les coniques*, Paris: broadside printed under the initials B. P., February 1640. Public Domain.

.. _cite-desargues-1639:

[desargues:1639] Brouillon project
----------------------------------

**Girard Desargues**, *Brouillon project d'une atteinte aux événemens des rencontres du cône avec un plan*, Paris, 1639.

.. _cite-apollonius-conics:

[apollonius:conics] Apollonii Pergaei quae Graece exstant
---------------------------------------------------------

**Apollonius of Perga**, *Conics* (Books I–VIII), ed. J. L. Heiberg, 2 vols., Leipzig: Teubner, 1891–1893.

.. _cite-pappus-synagoge:

[pappus:synagoge] Pappi Alexandrini Collectionis quae supersunt
---------------------------------------------------------------

**Pappus of Alexandria**, *Synagoge* (Mathematical Collection), ed. Friedrich Hultsch, 3 vols., Berlin: Weidmann, 1876–1878.

.. _cite-steiner-1832:

[steiner:1832] Systematische Entwicklung
----------------------------------------

**Jakob Steiner**, *Systematische Entwicklung der Abhängigkeit geometrischer Gestalten von einander*, Berlin: Fincke, 1832.

.. _cite-pluecker-1830:

[pluecker:1830] Über ein neues Übertragungsprincip
--------------------------------------------------

**Julius Plücker**, "Über ein neues Übertragungsprincip in der analytischen Geometrie", *Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik*, Vol. 5, pp. 268–286, 1830.

.. _cite-kirkman-1850:

[kirkman:1850] On the Hexagrammum Mysticum
------------------------------------------

**Thomas Penyngton Kirkman**, "On the Hexagrammum Mysticum", *Cambridge and Dublin Mathematical Journal*, Vol. 5, pp. 281–283, 1850.

.. _cite-chasles-1865:

[chasles:1865] Traité des sections coniques
-------------------------------------------

**Michel Chasles**, *Traité des sections coniques*, Paris: Gauthier-Villars, 1865.

.. _cite-smith-1929:

[smith:1929] A Source Book in Mathematics
-----------------------------------------

**David Eugene Smith** (ed.), *A Source Book in Mathematics*, New York: McGraw-Hill, 1929; reprint New York: Dover, 1959. Contains the English translation of Pascal's *Essay on Conics* by Dr. Frances Marguerite Clarke (pp. 326–330).
